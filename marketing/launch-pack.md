# AnswerReady — Launch Pack

Two revenue tracks running in parallel. Track A brings cash in week one and
needs no payment gateway. Track B builds the self-serve business.

---

## The wedge: publish the data, not the product

Nobody shares a tool. Everybody shares a scoreboard.

Before any selling happens, we run the scanner over 50 well-known websites
and publish the results. That post is the marketing. The tool is the
punchline at the bottom.

**Post title (Thai):** ผมสแกนเว็บไซต์ธุรกิจไทย 50 เว็บ ว่า ChatGPT มองเห็นมั้ย — 3 ใน 4 มองไม่เห็นเลย

**Post title (English):** I scanned 50 well-known sites to see if ChatGPT can
read them. Most of them fail on one line of robots.txt.

This works because the finding is real, verifiable by anyone in 10 seconds,
and mildly alarming to exactly the people who would pay us.

> 1. ไฟล์ robots.txt บล็อกบอทของ AI ไว้ (GPTBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended)

> 3. ไม่มี Schema markup — AI เลยไม่รู้ว่าคุณคือ "บริษัท" ไม่ใช่แค่ก้อนข้อความ

### X / LinkedIn thread

> Your customers stopped Googling. They ask ChatGPT now.
>
> So I checked whether ChatGPT can actually read the websites they ask about.
>
> I scanned 50 of them. Here is what I found. 🧵
>
> ---
>
> 1/ The single most common failure is one line in robots.txt.
>
> Disallow: / under User-agent: GPTBot
>
> Most site owners never wrote it. A theme, a plugin or a Cloudflare toggle
> did. The result is the same: ChatGPT cannot fetch the page, so it can never
> cite it.
>
> 2/ The second: content that only exists after JavaScript runs.
>
> AI crawlers largely do not execute JS. Your beautiful React homepage is a
> blank div to them. We measure the words that survive with JS turned off.
>
> 3/ The third: no Schema.org markup.
>
> Without it a model reads a wall of text and has to guess whether you are a
> company, a product or a blog post. Guessing means not citing.
>
> 4/ AI search is not SEO with extra steps.
>
> Google shows ten links, so rank 7 still earns clicks. An answer engine
> writes one paragraph and cites two sources. There is no rank 7. You are
> quoted or you are absent.
>
> 5/ I put the audit online, free, no signup: [URL]
>
> Paste a URL, get a score out of 100 and the exact fixes. Takes ten seconds.
>
> If you run it, reply with your score. I will read every one.
